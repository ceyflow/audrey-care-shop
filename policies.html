Our policies

Delivery

We deliver across Sri Lanka through our courier partners. The delivery fee, and the order value above which delivery is free, are shown in your bag and at checkout before you pay.

  • Orders are packed and dispatched as soon as they are confirmed. For bank transfers, that means once we’ve seen your payment.
  • Delivery times depend on your location. We’ll message you when your order is on its way.
  • Please make sure your address and mobile number are correct so the courier can reach you.
  • For cash on delivery orders, please have the full amount ready for the courier.

Returns & refunds

If something isn’t right with your order (damaged, leaking, or not what you ordered), contact us as soon as you can after delivery with your order number and a photo. We’ll make it right with a replacement or a refund.

  • For safety and hygiene reasons, we can’t take back opened personal care products unless they are faulty.
  • Refunds are made to the original payment method. Card refunds can take several working days to appear.
  • Bulk orders are made to order, so please talk to us about any issues straight away.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to take and deliver your order and to answer your questions: your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address and order details.

  • We use your details to process and deliver your order, send order updates, and reply to your messages. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we’ll also use your email for that.
  • We share your details only with the people who help us fulfil your order, such as our courier and, for card payments, our payment provider PayHere. Card details are entered on PayHere’s secure page and never reach us.
  • We don’t sell your personal data.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by contacting us.
  • This site stores your bag and, if you choose, your delivery details in your own browser to make checkout quicker.

Terms of sale

  • Prices are in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR). We do our best to keep prices and product information accurate, and may correct errors before confirming an order.
  • An order is accepted when we confirm it. We may decline or cancel an order, for example if an item is out of stock, and will refund any payment made.
  • Product photos are for illustration; packaging may vary slightly.
  • Bulk and wholesale supply is quoted separately and is subject to the terms in your quote.

Questions about any of this? Contact us.
